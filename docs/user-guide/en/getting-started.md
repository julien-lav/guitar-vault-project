# Getting started

Create your account, choose your language and find your way around Guitar Vault.

## Create an account

1. Open **Sign up** and enter your name, a unique username and your email address.
2. Choose a password with at least eight characters and a special character. Confirm it and submit the form.
3. Open the verification email and follow its link within 24 hours. You must verify your email before signing in.
4. Sign in with your **email address**, rather than your username.

If the link has expired, use the verification resend option on the login page. If you forget your password, choose **Forgot password?** and follow the email instructions. Password reset links expire; request a fresh one if yours no longer works.

## Find your next action

- **My space** opens your dashboard, profile, instruments, documents, transfers, invitations, workshop and requests.
- **Discover** opens articles and the studio, band and luthier directories. Some directories require sign-in.
- The **bell** opens your notifications. Open a notification to reach the relevant request or proposal.
- The language menu switches between **English** and **Français** and remembers your choice.
- **Help & guides** brings you back to these instructions, even when you are signed out.

Your dashboard shows your collection and shortcuts to your bands, studios and requests. Empty sections provide links to begin adding content.

## Set up your profile

Open your profile from **My space**, then choose **Edit my profile**. Add a display name, biography, activities, location and optional links or photo. Other signed-in members can view your musician profile. Your account email is not automatically published as contact information.
