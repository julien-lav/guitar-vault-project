# Documents, labels and archives

Store invoices and certificates, organise your own library and choose what accompanies a transfer.

## Upload and find a document

1. Open an instrument you own and choose **Instrument documents**.
2. Enter a title and category, select a PDF, JPEG or PNG up to 5 MB, and choose **Upload document**.
3. Open **My space → My documents** to find files you uploaded, received through an accepted transfer, or are allowed to read as report attachments.
4. Search by title or filename. Combine the instrument, category, access and library-view filters, then choose **Filter documents**. Use **Reset filters** to start again.

There is a limit of 20 uploaded files per contributor and instrument, including report attachments. Uploading a file does not automatically share it. **Download original file** returns the original upload.

## Change your library labels

Choose **Manage** beside a file. Edit the title or category and choose **Save my labels**. These labels belong to your library: the original file, report and transfer labels remain unchanged. Other readers keep their own labels.

## Archive and restore

Choose **Archive in my library** on the management page. The file disappears from your active library and from your new transfer selections. Existing downloads, reports and proposals remain available; other readers are unaffected.

To find it again, open **My documents**, select **Archived documents** or **All documents**, and apply the filters. Open **Manage → Restore to my library**. Archiving does not free an upload slot.

## Delete an unused upload

Only the uploader can delete a file that has never been attached to a report or selected for a transfer and has no previously granted access. If eligible, its management page offers **Delete unused upload**. Review the confirmation page and choose **Permanently delete this upload** only when you want to remove it for good. Deletion frees an upload slot.

A file referenced by a report or transfer is retained, even if the transfer is declined or cancelled. Archive it to tidy your library. If the page says the entry changed, reopen it before trying again.

## Understand access labels

- **Private**: available to the uploader.
- **Referenced in a transfer**: included in a stored transfer proposal. This label alone does not mean a recipient has accepted or gained access.
- **Request participants**: an attachment shared with its uploader and the original requester.
- **Report readers**: an attachment shared with its uploader, the original requester and the instrument’s current owner.

For the exact choices, read [ownership transfers](transfers.md) and [luthier reports](luthiers.md).
