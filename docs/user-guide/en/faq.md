# Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about privacy, access and common problems in Guitar Vault.

## Who can see my instruments?

Your collection is private to your account. Even an administrator cannot browse another member’s collection. Public workshop pages and articles, and member-visible profiles or studio pages, have their own visibility; do not put private information in published fields.

## Does “For sale” publish an advert?

No. It is a status in your private collection. A transfer requires a proposal to another active member and their acceptance.

## Does a verified luthier guarantee authenticity?

Workshop verification concerns professional identity. An instrument assessment is the named author’s opinion, with its own evidence and limitations. Read the report itself and any later corrections.

## Why can’t I delete a document?

You must be its uploader. Files attached to reports, referenced by transfers or covered by granted access are retained. A declined or cancelled transfer still keeps its references. You can archive the file in your personal library instead.

## Why did a renamed document keep its old title in a report?

Library labels are personal. Original labels remain in reports and transfer records so their evidence history is preserved. Other readers can choose different labels in their libraries.

## Does archiving revoke a buyer’s access?

No. Archiving affects your own library and new transfer choices. Existing proposals and download rights stay valid. Restore the document from the archived view whenever you need it again.

## Why can’t I transfer an instrument?

Check that the recipient’s exact username identifies an active member, that no other transfer is pending, and that pending luthier work has been completed or cancelled. You must be the current owner. Reopen the instrument if another action changed it while your form was open.

## Why didn’t I receive an email?

Check your spam folder and the address used at sign-up. Use the resend option for a new verification link. Password reset only sends mail for an eligible account, while showing the same confirmation either way. Invitations and transfer updates use in-app notifications; check the bell. On a local development instance, ask its operator whether mail is captured in Mailpit instead of delivered to your inbox.

## Why did a form stop working?

Your session may have expired, or the application may have restarted. Sign in again if needed, reopen the form and retry. For a document entry that changed in another tab, reopen its management page before resubmitting. After an upload validation error, select the file again.

## Can I pay, book a studio or sign a sale contract here?

The app currently records requests, discussions, proposed amounts and ownership handovers. It does not process payments, reserve studio availability or manage sale contracts. A luthier appointment can be confirmed when the other participant agrees to the latest proposed time; find this in **My appointments**.
