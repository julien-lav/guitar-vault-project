# Your instruments and wishlist

Keep a private collection with photos, identifying details and personal notes.

## Add an instrument

1. Open **My space → My instruments**.
2. Choose **Add an instrument**.
3. Enter its name or model, brand and status. Add its year and serial number if you know them.
4. Add personal notes, history, previous-owner details, recordings or concerts where useful.
5. Choose any photos, then save. Open the instrument from your collection to check its details.

Photos must be JPEG or PNG, up to 5 MB each; you can keep up to five photos per instrument. If validation fails, correct the fields and select the files again before saving.

Your collection belongs to your account. Another member, including an administrator, cannot browse it. Setting the status to **For sale** does not publish a marketplace listing.

## Keep a wishlist

Open **My space → My wishlist** to add an instrument you would like to own. You can edit or remove wishlist entries. This list is private and separate from instruments you own.

## Build the passport

From an instrument, open its documents to add files, request a luthier intervention, or read professional passport reports. Before handing it to another member, use the transfer proposal workflow rather than creating a duplicate instrument.

Read [documents](documents.md), [luthier reports](luthiers.md) and [ownership transfers](transfers.md) for the sharing rules.

## Vintage shops and public sale listings

Open **Discover → Vintage shops** to search shops by name or location. Each shop publishes its contact details, visiting arrangements and available or reserved instruments with asking prices. Open a listing to read its sale description, see its explicitly published photo, ask about the instrument, or request a shop appointment. Sold instruments leave the catalogue. Publication is supplied by the operator and does not verify professional identity or authenticity.

**My space → My vintage shop** lets a member create a shop profile and publish it. Choose **Add or edit a shop instrument** to select a passport you own or create a new instrument in your collection by entering its name, brand and optional year. Add a public description, asking price and currency (EUR, USD, GBP, CAD or CHF), an optional JPEG/PNG photo up to 5 MB, stock status and publication choice. A listing starts as a draft unless you select publication. The shop must also be published. Open a listing in your inventory to edit its price, description, public photo and stock status, or unpublish it. Only one live published listing per instrument is allowed.

Only the name, brand, year and sale details entered for the listing become public. Serial numbers, private notes, collection photos, files and reports stay private. A listing photo is a separate upload; it is never copied from the collection. The private passport link remains available to the owner. An accepted ownership transfer hides the previous shop’s listing and photo from public access; it does not give that shop access to the buyer’s private collection. Marking stock sold does not transfer ownership: arrange the sale and payment with the buyer, then use the existing [ownership transfer](transfers.md) to hand over the passport and explicitly selected documents.

## Contact a shop or arrange a viewing

Sign in to contact a shop, either generally or about a particular listing. A simple enquiry needs no instrument of your own or appointment time. Follow it in **My space → My shop enquiries and appointments**. The shop receives an in-app notification. A listing enquiry retains the instrument name and asking price at the time of the request; later price changes do not rewrite it or constitute a sale agreement.

Either participant can propose a future date and time with an explicit time zone; the other must confirm or decline. Confirmed visits last 30 minutes, and overlapping visits at the same shop cannot both be confirmed. Pending proposals do not reserve time. Cancellation frees the slot and keeps the conversation history. To change a confirmed visit, create a new request and cancel the old one. Ambiguous and nonexistent times during clock changes are rejected.

Messages and appointments are private to the original visitor and shop operator, including after the shop is unpublished or the instrument changes owner. Administrators do not receive access to these conversations. An enquiry or viewing does not buy or reserve stock. Agree final price, payment and practical visiting arrangements directly with the shop.
