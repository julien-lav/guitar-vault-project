# Bands, shows and studios

Collaborate with other members and organise requests without exposing your private instrument collection.

## Create or join a band

Open **Discover → Bands** to browse by genre, region or city. Create a band, fill in its presentation and invite musicians by exact username. Invited musicians accept or decline from **My space → Invitations**. An invitation alone does not add a member.

The creator manages the band profile and invitations. Current members can leave, and the creator can remove members after confirmation. The creator remains the band’s manager and member. A band can have up to 50 members, counting pending invitations toward the limit.

## Plan dates and shows

Open a band profile to manage its connections, show invitations and date proposals when you are its manager. The other manager must accept a connection or invitation. Accepting a proposed date does not automatically publish a show or reserve a calendar slot; create the show separately when ready.

The **Private calendar** on a band profile is available only to current members. The manager creates and edits events; members can read them. Weekly repeating events require an end date. Editing or deleting a series affects all its occurrences, including past ones.

## Find a studio or publish your own

Open **Discover → Studios**, use the location and price filters and read a studio’s services, rates and contact details. Pricing filters exclude studios with pricing on request when a minimum or maximum price is set.

Any active member can create a studio profile and manage the ones they created. Published contact details are visible on the studio profile; your account email is not inserted automatically.

## Request a studio quote

1. Open the studio profile and choose **Request a quote**.
2. Describe the project and add your preferred dates and budget if useful.
3. Follow the conversation in **My space → My requests**.
4. Reply in the conversation; the studio manager can include a proposed amount. Earlier messages and proposals remain in the history.

The quote conversation is private to the requester and studio manager. A quote or proposed price does not create a booking, payment or calendar reservation.
